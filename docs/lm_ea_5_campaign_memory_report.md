# LM-EA-5 — campaign memory and endings report

**Build:** `0.3.0-alpha.354`

LM-EA-5 brings the runtime to twenty authored event or meeting decisions. Its two new meetings live on failure-forward roads rather than interrupting the reliable routes:

- **Lamps in the Ash Chapel** asks whether to establish a marked refuge chain or strip the abandoned bell and lamp oil.
- **Water Under the Lee** asks whether to spend a reserve marking a public cistern or seal the water into a private ledger.

A surviving public choice earns a stable regional-development ID. On a later run, the Cinder Refuge Chain adds a third response to Coals for the Climb; Shared Cisterns add a third response at the White Horizon. These are inspectable event options with explicit resource effects, not hidden global bonuses.

Cinder's **Redundant Lift** and Salt's **Dependency Watch** are optional replay orders. Each has two stated solutions and grants no currency, stat increase, or unlock. Their purpose is to ask the player to test a different viable plan.

Debrief now composes three independent facets—survival, regional direction, and promise state—into a named ending record. The current authored axes support 60 combinations while retaining the chapter's specific terminal result. Tests cover both new events, later-run options, multiple developments from one run, local profile persistence, replay-goal evaluation, ending composition, and Debrief presentation. Human testing is still required to determine whether the new causal records are memorable and emotionally legible.
