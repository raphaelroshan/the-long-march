# The Long March — Early Access Decision Record

**Decision:** Target Early Access as a small campaign of distinct, complete fortress journeys rather than only two polished chapter demonstrations or an unfinished continent.

Ashgate Lowlands and Flooded Veyru remain the quality anchors. Two additional regions may be visually lighter and reuse the fortress renderer, temporary art, audio, and transition components. They must still have a distinct route promise, settlement identity, threat pressure, recovery choice, failure-forward development, save-safe completion, and terminal record.

The principal trade-off is **campaign breadth versus final asset completeness**. We accept modular or temporary regional art, compact event sets, and a limited number of chassis/modules in the follow-on regions. We do not accept decorative background swaps, repeated encounter tables, meaningless settlements, or routes that do not change the fortress decision.

The second trade-off is **operational complexity versus comprehension**. The fortress may accumulate dependencies and scars, but every new system must be visible through route promise, forecast, target, impact, recovery, and Debrief language. Audio, particles, and transition animation remain presentation layers and cannot alter route results, damage, heat, fuel, pressure, targeting, or saves.

The active implementation order is anchor completion, a third region, a fourth region and alternate chassis, module/specialist/threat breadth, event/regional-memory/replay depth, and release hardening. Human testing may later calibrate the build but is not a prerequisite for implementation. Deterministic replay, complete-journey fixtures, responsive and input checks, screenshots, package validation, and save migration evidence are the active gates.
