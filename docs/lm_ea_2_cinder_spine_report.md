# LM-EA-2 — Cinder Spine completion report

**Build:** `0.3.0-alpha.351`  
**Scope:** Third-region vertical slice

LM-EA-2 adds The Cinder Spine as a complete five-contact chapter from Blackkiln to Switchback Commune. It includes a heat-bearing dynamo contract, six valid route plans across three post-recovery choices, three regional threat families, two mass-sensitive climbs, chained lift/community decisions, Old Lift recovery, a pressure-closed tunnel with a guaranteed refuge bypass, three terminal results, and the persistent Communal Lift Plan.

The title menu, March Charter, Continue metadata, route map, settlement presenter, travel scene, contact scene, current-order guidance, run record, and Debrief now recognize the chapter. Blackkiln uses a forge-bazaar presentation, Cinder travel uses a volcanic palette and eight route landmarks, and its threats expose their own wind-ups, targets, counters, and failure risks.

Verification includes a structural JSON validator, two materially distinct complete command-only plans with 34 exact save/load checkpoints, a player-command UI flow, 1280×720 and 1600×900 large-text/reduced-motion profiles, and the unchanged full Ashgate/Veyru regression suite.

Known limitation: the chapter uses the shared procedural fortress and temporary vector/kit visual language. Human comprehension, balance, encounter pacing, and art recognition still require consented playtests; automated tests make no claim about those qualities.
