# LM-EA-4 — dependency systems report

**Build:** `0.3.0-alpha.353`

LM-EA-4 expands fortress planning with two staffed module families, two facility-bound specialists, and two threats that ask different questions of the same chassis.

- The **Field Infirmary** and **Command Deck** both need stable power and physical adjacency to operational Crew Quarters. Losing the crew room switches the facility—and its specialist contribution—off.
- **Sela Vonn** turns Run Hot into an explicit schedule bargain: one fewer travel day, four additional risk points, and a command feint against scouts and Signal Hunters.
- **Dr. Nera Quill** reduces incoming crew and refuge damage by one while the infirmary remains staffed.
- **Signal Hunters** expose signal, command, and exterior systems. **Bridgebreakers** attack the lower hull, engines, and armor.
- The **Salvage Crane** uses power, two vertical cells, and an exterior mount to brace Bridgebreaker approaches. It competes directly with roof weapons and signals instead of becoming free defense.

The Saltglass hiring post explains both candidates and refuses assignment until the correct facility is Ready. Stable IDs, authored content, threat presentation, dependency cards, save rejection, exact round-trip persistence, and deterministic counter damage are automated. Balance and whether players understand the facility trade without prompting remain human playtest questions.
