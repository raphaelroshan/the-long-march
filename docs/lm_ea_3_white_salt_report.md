# LM-EA-3 — White Salt Expanse and Salt Skimmer report

**Build:** `0.3.0-alpha.352`

LM-EA-3 adds the fourth complete five-contact region and the first alternate fortress chassis. The White Salt Expanse includes Saltglass Haven, The Windbreak, six complete route plans, ash-front closure pressure, a guaranteed Lee Trench fallback, public-versus-private signal consequences, an escort-versus-race commitment, three regional threats, three results, and persistent Public Salt Beacons.

The Salt Skimmer has a 6×4 presentation grid with two unavailable lower-corner cells, a 13-mass limit, and three exterior mounts. Placement and save validation enforce those constraints, while the workbench renders the missing cells explicitly.

Automated acceptance covers two complete command-only plans with 30 save/load checkpoints, direct player-command launch and route flow, default and responsive layouts, content validation, and the full earlier chapter regression suite. Human comprehension and balance remain unproven until observed playtests.
