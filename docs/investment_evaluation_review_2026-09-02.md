## Investment assessment

The Long March has the strongest creative premise and opening identity, but the investment case depends on demonstrating that the moving fortress remains compelling after the tutorial. An evaluator needs to see route commitment, travel pressure, contact or event staging, damage, recovery, settlement consequence, and Debrief—not only a strong introduction.

The full creative vertical should be one complete authored journey from First Watch through terminal Debrief with a memorable fortress, distinct settlements, meaningful routes, specialist conflict, a named contact, a recoverable crisis, a regional consequence, and an alternate chassis or doctrine choice. The wider Early Access skeleton should preserve four regions while ensuring each region has a different operational question.

The complete implementation contract is [`investment_evaluation_roadmap.md`](investment_evaluation_roadmap.md). The first task is **LM-I1: creative vertical lock**, followed by a third region with a new route promise and failure-forward development, then a fourth-region/chassis comparison. Do not expand the continent with background-only regions or generic encounters.
