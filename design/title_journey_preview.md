# Title journey preview

## Player problem

The title offers Ashgate, Flooded Veyru, and a saved Continue slot, but button names alone do not explain why a player would choose one over another. The previous right-hand card described both chapters at once, so keyboard, controller, and mouse users could focus a concrete action while seeing generic information that did not belong to that action.

## Interaction contract

- Focusing or hovering Guided Ashgate, Quick Ashgate, Flooded Veyru, Continue, or save recovery updates the existing right-hand card in place.
- Keyboard/controller focus is authoritative. A temporary mouse hover may preview another action, but leaving it restores the focused action's card.
- Ashgate names its guided or quick-start contract, Blockade Watch pressure, Morrowline recovery, and Siege Beast finale.
- Flooded Veyru names its medicine carrier, Rising Water pressure, Evacuation Camp recovery, and Civic Guardian finale. Public Archive Signal appears only when that development is active.
- Continue names the exact saved region, age, phase, next decision, fortress condition, creating build, and replacement boundary.
- An unusable primary save explains whether a validated backup exists and keeps new chapter starts visibly separate from recovery.
- Pressing a start action still starts or confirms immediately. The preview does not add an extra click, modal, or hidden selection state.

## Scope boundary

This is title presentation over existing authored chapters and validated save metadata. It does not change region balance, starting state, route visibility, save schema, campaign progression, or the existing confirmation rules around replacing Continue.

## Required evidence

- Mouse hover and keyboard/controller focus select the same action-specific copy.
- Mouse exit restores the currently focused action.
- Fresh-start, valid Continue, unusable-save recovery, and Public Archive Signal states have deterministic shell coverage.
- Ashgate and Veyru previews remain readable at 1280×720, including 110% text and High contrast.
