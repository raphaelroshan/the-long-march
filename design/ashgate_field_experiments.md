# Ashgate Field Experiments

## Purpose

Field experiments give a returning player one visible question to test without changing campaign rewards, difficulty math, or unlocks. They are optional and selected at Ashgate before the first road.

## Quarry Adaptation

**Proof:** Secure Cinder Quarry.

Two validated solutions:

1. Use Run Hot with enough movement and firepower to end the mixed contact quickly.
2. Use Protect Cargo with lower-hull armor to absorb the Burrower while the weapon handles the raiders.

## Signal Discipline

**Proof:** Secure Signal Causeway.

Two validated solutions:

1. Restore Broken Relay and recruit Iven Pell for reliable forecasting.
2. Carry and preserve an operational Wall Lamp without recruiting Iven.

## Rules

- Selecting an order changes no resources or combat values.
- An order can be switched only at Ashgate before the first road.
- The stable order ID and live proof state survive save/load.
- Planning and recovery retain the order; Debrief records Proven or Incomplete.
- There is no mastery currency, reward track, unlock gate, or daily rotation.
