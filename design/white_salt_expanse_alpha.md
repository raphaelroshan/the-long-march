# White Salt Expanse — Early Access chapter contract

The fourth chapter crosses open salt flats where cover is absent, signals travel far, and water is the primary operational constraint. Saltglass Haven offers a visible-beacon escort for the Refugee Compact. Accepting it makes rival scouts harder but improves recovery and enables the strongest alliance result.

The alternate `salt_skimmer` chassis retains the readable 6×4 grid while cutting away both lower corners, reducing mass capacity from 14 to 13, and increasing exterior capacity from two mounts to three. This creates a genuine layout choice: stronger signal and weapon coverage on a lighter, less forgiving deck.

The route graph branches through Buried Observatory or Quiet Caravan, converges at The Windbreak, then offers Salt Mine, Empty Mile, Beacon Road, or the pressure-opened Lee Trench. At whiteout pressure, Empty Mile closes and Lee Trench opens, preserving a low-value recovery route. Rival Approach establishes the final escort-or-race doctrine before the Salt Citadel contact.

Publicly broadcasting the observatory beacons and surviving earns `salt_public_beacons`. Later runs reveal Salt Mine contacts without reducing their risk. The three outcomes are `expanse_allied`, `expanse_crossed`, and `salt_lost`.
