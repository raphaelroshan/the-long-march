# Cinder Quarry Route Slice

## Player question

Is the fortress prepared to defend cargo above and the movement chain below in the same contact, in exchange for a guaranteed field repair before Meridian Pass?

## Stable content

- Node ID: `cinder_quarry`
- Route: Morrowline Camp → Cinder Quarry → Meridian Pass
- Cost: 1 day, 2 fuel, +1 blockade pressure
- Base risk: 48% before existing state modifiers
- Reward: 18 Ashmarks
- Forecast: raiders above and movement below
- Encounter order: Road Raider, then Burrower

## Counterplay and weakness

A weapon-first Run Hot plan can break both contacts before the Burrower completes repeated engine attacks, but accepts overheat damage. A Protect Cargo plan with a Side Armor Skirt beside the movement chain can survive the longer exchange without Run Hot. Signal forecasting lowers encounter pressure; carrying neither offense nor lower-hull protection leaves the engine exposed.

## Recovery consequence

Victory restores up to two durability to the deterministic weakest damaged installed system. If no installed system is damaged, the plate stock sells for eight Ashmarks. The consequence appears in the normal road report and arrival receipt; it adds no save field or alternate economy.

## Teaching and evidence

The route table states the full recovery payoff before Commit. The contact combines two already-taught threat families, and the arrival receipt names the repaired system and exact before/after durability. Automated coverage proves two viable plans, same-save replay, stable enemy order, route availability, and the onward Meridian connection.
