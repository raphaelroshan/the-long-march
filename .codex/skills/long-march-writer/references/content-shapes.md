# Content shapes

These are drafting constraints, not new schemas. Inspect the target presenter and tests before applying them.

## Event or specialist choice

- Stable ID: existing `snake_case` namespace.
- Title: 2–7 concrete words.
- Setup: usually 25–70 words; establish object, pressure, and decision.
- Choice label: lead with a verb; usually 3–10 words.
- Requirements and effects: structured, exact, and implementation-backed.
- Visible result: usually one or two sentences naming immediate receipt and exposed cost.
- Later callback: a changed road, service, shortage, refuge, relationship, or ending facet.

Drafting frame:

```text
TITLE
Specific object or person + present failure/need + why the fortress is involved.

[VERB + THING]
Cost/requirement: exact structured value
Result: what is preserved; what becomes harder
Later: where this can return
```

## Route card

Name destination and expose days, fuel, risk or threat family, visibility/confidence, assignment relevance, closure consequence, and recovery path. Keep inspection distinct from selection and selection distinct from Commit. Do not smuggle an undisclosed event into atmospheric copy.

## Settlement station

Give the station a visible purpose, current local constraint, exact service cost, restored system or gained information, remaining service count, and what choosing it prevents. Place identity should change the framing and objects, not the command semantics.

## Contact forecast and receipt

Before contact, distinguish known threat, likely target, uncertainty, and available counter. After impact, name attacker, target, actual damage, mitigation source, dependency consequence, and next response. Prose may stage cause and effect but cannot change timing or target order.

## Tutorial

Teach one operation at a time in this order: goal, relevant state, required action, visible result, then why it mattered. Never claim the player understood. Provide replayable help and do not block required input behind lore.

## Debrief

Summarize what happened, why it happened, what was carried or broken, which promise held, and what changed beyond the immediate run. Prefer bounded facts and causal arrows over a moral score. Endings describe an operating philosophy and its beneficiaries and costs, not a hidden alignment judgment.

## Quiet road beat

A quiet beat still needs one authored anchor: maintenance work, a recognizable landmark, crew routine, a consequence passing in view, or preparation for a forecast threat. Keep it brief. Silence and ordinary work are contrast, not filler.

## Final pass

Read each string without its surrounding lore. Confirm that:

- the player knows what can be acted on;
- the cost appears before commitment;
- the result does not promise more than state can produce;
- two choices preserve different legitimate priorities;
- at least one concrete noun makes the passage region- or fortress-specific;
- deleting any decorative sentence would not improve it;
- title, button, body, and receipt do not repeat the same sentence four times.
